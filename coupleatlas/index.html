CoupleAtlas Support

Keep your days together, the places you visit, and the people and pets in your story. CoupleAtlas is for iPhone with iOS 17 or later. Your library stays on your device; there is no account or partner sync.

Getting started

Enter your two names and the date you got together. Tap “Record a moment” to add a date, gift, trip, or another moment. Pick photos or a video, choose a place, and save. Open the globe to revisit a city and its places.

Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets

Add CoupleAtlas from your iPhone’s widget gallery. Home Screen widgets show your days together; the medium widget also shows your globe and next celebration. Lock Screen widgets show days and the next celebration. If a widget asks you to begin, open the app and set up your profile. After changing your profile, open the app again; iOS controls when widget updates appear. Sensitive text can be hidden by your Lock Screen privacy settings.

Photos, video, and weather

Only the photos and videos you select are copied into the app. Trim videos longer than three minutes in Photos before importing. When available, selected media’s date and location help fill in a memory. Current location is used only when you tap that option.

Automatic weather uses Apple Weather and needs a network connection. If it is unavailable, your memory still saves. You can add weather manually or switch automatic weather off in Settings. Manually entered weather is kept.

Exporting and deleting

Settings → Export backup creates a ZIP with your records and copied media. Save it somewhere you control before deleting your library or changing phones. Backups may contain names, dates, locations, photos, and videos, so choose their destination carefully. There is currently no in-app backup import. Share a poster from the Poster tab using the system share sheet.

Settings → Delete all data removes the local library, copied media, caches, and widget snapshot. It does not delete originals in Photos or files and posters you already exported. The app does not maintain a server copy for recovery.

Questions and problems

Write to hello@arete-robotics.com with your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, and steps to reproduce the issue. Please leave private memories out of screenshots you send.

CoupleAtlas 支持

记住在一起的日子、走过的地方，以及故事里的家人和宠物。适用于 iOS 17 及以上版本的 iPhone。资料保存在本机，没有账号，也没有伴侣同步。

开始记录

填写两人的名字和在一起的日期。点击“记一笔”，添加约会、礼物、旅行或其他回忆；选照片或视频、填地点后保存。在地球上打开城市，重温具体地点。

桌面与锁屏小组件

在 iPhone 的小组件图库添加 CoupleAtlas。桌面小组件显示在一起的天数，中号还显示地球和下一个纪念日；锁屏小组件显示天数或下一个纪念日。若提示先开始，请打开 App 建立资料。修改资料后再打开一次 App；实际刷新时间由 iOS 控制。锁屏隐私设置可以隐藏敏感文字。

照片、视频和天气

App 只复制你选中的照片和视频。超过三分钟的视频请先在“照片”里剪短。媒体中已有的拍摄时间和位置可用于填写回忆；只有点击“当前位置”时才使用设备定位。

自动天气通过 Apple Weather 获取，需要联网。天气不可用时回忆仍能保存，可以手动填写，也可在设置中关闭自动天气。手动填写的天气会保留。

导出与删除

在设置中“导出备份”，生成含记录和已复制媒体的 ZIP。删除资料或更换手机前，请先保存到自己控制的位置。备份可能包含名字、日期、地点、照片和视频，分享前请确认接收位置。目前没有 App 内导入备份功能。海报可在海报页通过系统分享面板导出。

设置中的“删除全部数据”会删除本机资料、已复制媒体、缓存和小组件快照；不会删除“照片”中的原件，也不会撤回已经导出的文件和海报。App 没有可用于恢复的服务器副本。
