CoupleAtlas Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

CoupleAtlas keeps your relationship profile and memories on your iPhone. It has no account, developer-operated server, analytics, tracking, or advertising. Arete Robotics does not receive your library.

What stays on your device

Your names, relationship date, family and pet details, anniversaries, memories, locations, weather, and selected photos and videos are saved in the app’s local storage. The app copies and processes only media you choose in Apple’s picker; it does not scan your whole photo library. Selected media’s capture time and GPS metadata may be used to fill in a memory. Original media in Photos is unchanged.

A small snapshot of names, relationship date, upcoming celebrations, place coordinates, and counts is shared locally with the app’s widget extension through an App Group. The widget does not read your memory database or media. Names and celebration text are marked as privacy-sensitive for system redaction. Notification reminders are scheduled on the device if you enable them.

Local preferences remember the setup day and which app versions have requested a review. These preferences are not sent to us. Your device’s backup settings may include app data in backups managed by Apple.

Apple maps, location, and weather

Apple Maps provides map display, place search, nearby suggestions, and geocoding. Queries and relevant coordinates are sent to Apple to provide those features, including coordinates from selected media when resolving a place. The app requests device location only when you choose Current location; it does not track your location in the background.

If automatic weather is enabled, the app sends Apple Weather a memory’s date and approximate coordinates rounded to 0.01 degrees (roughly a kilometre, varying by latitude). It may also ask Apple’s geocoder for the time zone at those approximate coordinates, and download Apple Weather attribution images and links. Names, notes, photos, and videos are not sent for weather. You can disable automatic weather and enter it manually. Apple’s services operate under Apple’s privacy terms.

Export, sharing, and support

Backups and posters leave the app only when you choose to export, save, or share them through system controls. The destination you choose handles the exported data under its own terms. We receive no automatic copy. If you email support, we receive the message and attachments you choose to send.

Deleting your data

Delete an individual memory, or use Settings → Delete all data to clear the library, copied media, caches, and widget snapshot. Local review-request preferences remain. Deletion does not remove original photos and videos or files you previously exported to other destinations. We do not keep a server copy.

CoupleAtlas 隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 10 月 30 日

CoupleAtlas 将情侣资料和回忆保存在你的 iPhone 上。没有账号、开发者运营的服务器、统计、跟踪或广告。Arete Robotics 不会收到你的资料库。

保存在本机的内容

名字、在一起的日期、家人和宠物资料、纪念日、回忆、地点、天气，以及你选中的照片和视频，都保存在 App 本地存储中。App 只复制和处理你通过苹果选择器选中的媒体，不会扫描整个照片图库。所选媒体已有的拍摄时间和 GPS 信息可能用于填写回忆；“照片”中的原件不会被修改。

一份包含名字、在一起的日期、即将到来的纪念日、地点坐标和统计数的小快照，通过 App Group 在本机共享给小组件扩展。小组件不读取回忆数据库或媒体。名字和纪念日文字标记为敏感内容，可由系统隐藏。开启提醒后，通知在本机安排。

本地偏好记录首次设置日期和已经请求评分的 App 版本，不会发给我们。根据设备备份设置，App 数据可能包含在苹果管理的设备备份中。

苹果地图、定位与天气

地图显示、地点搜索、附近建议和地理编码由苹果地图提供。为完成这些功能，搜索词和相关坐标会发送给苹果；根据所选媒体定位地点时，也可能包含媒体中的坐标。只有点击“当前位置”时才请求设备定位，不会在后台跟踪位置。

开启自动天气时，App 会将回忆日期和取整到 0.01 度的大致坐标发送给 Apple Weather（约一公里，随纬度变化）；必要时用这些大致坐标向苹果地理编码查询时区，并下载 Apple Weather 标注图片和链接。不会为获取天气发送名字、备注、照片或视频。可以关闭自动天气，改为手动填写。苹果服务适用苹果自己的隐私条款。

导出、分享和支持

只有你通过系统控件主动导出、保存或分享时，备份和海报才会离开 App，适用所选接收服务的条款。我们不会自动收到副本。如果你发送支持邮件，我们会收到你主动发送的内容和附件。

删除数据

可以删除单条回忆，或在设置中“删除全部数据”，清空资料库、已复制媒体、缓存和小组件快照。本地评分请求偏好仍保留。删除不会移除原始照片和视频，也不会删除已经导出到其他位置的文件。我们没有服务器副本。
