Monthlet · Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026

Monthlet ("the app") is provided by Arete Robotics. The app keeps a child's day count and age, "firsts", height and weight records and cities visited, and turns photos into monthly milestone cards.

1. Who uses the app

The app is used by parents and guardians. It is not designed for children and is not in the Kids category. A child's name, birthday, photos, height, weight and cities visited are entered by the parent or guardian.

2. We do not collect your information

  • Everything you enter or add in the app (profiles, photos, firsts, growth records, cards, footprints and settings) is stored only on your device.
  • We have no server and no accounts. We do not upload, receive or see any of this information.
  • The app has no ads, no analytics, no third-party SDKs and no tracking.

3. Children's information

Your child's information is entered by you and stays on your device. The app does not send it to us or to anyone else. (Under Chinese law, personal information of minors under 14 is sensitive personal information; the app does not transmit it to the developer or any third party.)

4. Network use: city search and App Store purchase

When you search for a city in Footprints, the app uses Apple Maps (Apple MapKit) and sends only the text you type to Apple. Apple's privacy policy applies to Apple's handling of it. The city name, country and coordinates of the result you pick are saved on your device. The app does not ask for location permission and does not read your device's location.

Apart from that, only buying and restoring All Templates goes through Apple's App Store (see section 5).

5. One-time in-app purchase: All Templates

  • The app is free to use, and 4 card templates are free. All Templates is a one-time in-app purchase (not a subscription) that unlocks the other templates and every template added later.
  • Buying, payment and restoring purchases are handled entirely by Apple's App Store. The developer does not receive and cannot see any payment information, including your card, Apple Account or contact details.
  • The app only asks the App Store, on your device, whether the Apple Account on this device has bought All Templates. It does not send purchase records to us or to anyone else. Apple's privacy policy applies to Apple's handling of this data.

6. Permissions

  • Add to Photos (add only): used only when you tap Save to Photos, to save a card you made. The app cannot see your photo library.
  • Notifications: asked only when you turn on reminders, to schedule reminders on your device.
  • Photos are chosen with the system photo picker; the app only receives the photo you pick and does not need photo library access.

7. Widgets

Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets show your baby's name, day count and age; the medium widget may show a small photo. This data is kept in a storage area on your device shared only between the app and its widget, and does not leave the device. Lock Screen widgets can be visible while the device is locked; remove them if you prefer.

8. Sharing and backups

  • When you share a card or poster, the image goes through the system share sheet to the place you choose, and that place's rules apply.
  • Export a backup creates a ZIP file with your records and photos on your device; you decide where to save or send it. Keep backup files safe.
  • Whether your device backups (for example iCloud Backup or a computer backup) include the app's data depends on your own system settings.

9. Export and deletion

  • Settings → Export a backup: exports all records and photos.
  • Settings → Restore from a backup: merges a backup back into the app.
  • Settings → Delete all data: deletes all profiles, photos, records and cards in the app (two confirmations). Deleting the app also deletes this data.
  • Images you saved to Photos or shared elsewhere are not affected; delete them there.

10. Changes

If the way the app handles data changes, we will update this policy and the in-app notice and change the effective date.

11. Contact

宝宝时光轴 · 隐私政策

生效日期： 2026 年 11 月 20 日

宝宝时光轴（以下称“本 App”）由 Arete Robotics 提供。本 App 用来记录孩子的天数和月龄、“第一次”、身高体重和去过的城市，并把照片做成月龄卡片。

1. 谁在使用本 App

本 App 由父母或监护人使用，不面向儿童，也不在儿童类别中。孩子的姓名、生日、照片、身高体重、去过的城市等信息由监护人自己填写。

2. 我们不收集你的信息

  • 你在本 App 里填写和添加的所有内容（宝宝档案、照片、“第一次”、成长记录、卡片、足迹、设置）只保存在你的设备上。
  • 我们没有服务器，没有账号系统，不上传、不接收、也看不到这些信息。
  • 本 App 没有广告，没有统计或分析工具，没有第三方 SDK，不做跟踪。

3. 未成年人信息

根据中国法律，不满十四周岁未成年人的个人信息属于敏感个人信息。本 App 中孩子的信息由监护人填写，只保存在监护人的设备上，本 App 不向开发者或任何第三方传输这些信息。

4. 联网：城市搜索和 App Store 购买

在“足迹”里搜索城市时，本 App 使用苹果地图（Apple MapKit）搜索，只把你输入的文字发给苹果。苹果对这些数据的处理适用苹果的隐私政策。搜索结果中你选中的城市名、国家和坐标保存在你的设备上。本 App 不申请定位权限，不读取设备位置。

除此之外，只有“模板全集”的购买和恢复购买通过苹果的 App Store 进行（见第 5 节）。

5. 一次性内购“模板全集”

  • 本 App 可以免费使用，其中 4 套卡片模板免费。“模板全集”是一个一次性内购（不是订阅），解锁其余模板和以后新增的模板。
  • 购买、付款和恢复购买全部由苹果的 App Store 处理。开发者收不到、也看不到任何付款信息，包括你的银行卡、Apple 账户和联系方式。
  • 本 App 只在你的设备上向 App Store 确认这台设备上的 Apple 账户是否买过“模板全集”，不把购买记录发给我们或任何第三方。苹果对这些数据的处理适用苹果的隐私政策。

6. 权限

  • 存到相册（仅添加）：只在你点“存到相册”时，把你做好的卡片存进相册。本 App 看不到你的相册。
  • 通知：只在你打开“纪念日提醒我”时申请，用于在本机安排纪念日提醒。
  • 选择照片使用系统的照片选择器，本 App 只能拿到你选中的那一张，不需要相册读取权限。

7. 小组件

主屏幕和锁屏小组件显示宝宝的名字、天数和月龄，中号小组件可能显示一张缩小的照片。这些数据存放在你设备上只供本 App 和它的小组件使用的共享空间里，不离开设备。锁屏可能在设备锁定时显示这些内容，如不需要可以移除锁屏小组件。

8. 分享和备份

  • 你分享卡片或海报时，图片通过系统分享面板发往你选择的地方，之后的处理适用该去处的规则。
  • “导出备份”在你的设备上生成一个 ZIP 文件，其中包含你的记录和照片，由你决定保存或发送到哪里。请妥善保管备份文件。
  • 你的设备备份（例如 iCloud 云备份或电脑备份）是否包含本 App 的数据，取决于你自己的系统设置。

9. 导出和删除

  • 设置 → 导出备份：导出全部记录和照片。
  • 设置 → 从备份恢复：把备份合并回本 App。
  • 设置 → 删除全部数据：删除本 App 里的全部档案、照片、记录和卡片（需要确认两次）。删除本 App 也会删除这些数据。
  • 已经存到系统相册或分享出去的图片不受影响，需要你自己在相册或去处删除。

10. 变更

如果本 App 的数据处理方式发生变化，我们会更新本政策和 App 内的说明，并修改生效日期。
