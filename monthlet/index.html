Monthlet · Help

Monthlet: count the days, keep the firsts, and turn a photo into a monthly card worth sharing. No sign-up, no ads, photos stay on your iPhone.

Questions

How are days and age counted?

The day your baby was born is day 1. Months follow the calendar: born on 10 January, 1 month on 10 February. A baby born on the 31st counts the last day of shorter months; a baby born on 29 February counts 28 February in other years.

Which special days are there?

1 week, each month, 100 days, the half birthday, birthdays, 500 days and 1,000 days. Turn on Settings → Remind me of special days to get a notification at 9:00 the day before and on the day.

How do I make a monthly card?

On Today, tap Make a monthly card and choose a photo. The title is filled in from the photo date (today if the photo has none). Change the template and size (3:4, 1:1, 9:16), drag and pinch the photo, edit the title, name, date and a line, then share or save to Photos.

Can I remove the small "Made with Monthlet" line?

Yes: Settings → turn off "Show 'Made with' on cards".

Which templates are free? What is All Templates?

4 templates are free (Airy, Washi tape, Red seal, Dial), and so is everything else: the day count, widgets, firsts, growth records, footprints and backups. All Templates is a one-time in-app purchase, not a subscription: buy it once to use the other templates and every template added later. You can preview a template with a lock using your own photo; you are asked to buy only when you share it or save it to Photos. Payment is handled by Apple's App Store; we do not receive and cannot see any payment information.

New phone or reinstalled the app: how do I get All Templates back?

Signed in with the same Apple Account, go to Settings → All Templates → Restore Purchases. The purchase page also has Restore Purchases.

What do the height and weight lines mean?

They join the numbers you entered so you can look back. They are records only, not medical advice; ask a doctor if you have concerns.

Two children?

Settings → Add a baby. Tap the name on Today to switch. Each widget can show a different child: edit the widget and choose.

New phone, or a copy for family?

Settings → Export a backup gives you a ZIP file. Install the app on the other phone and use Settings → Restore from a backup. Restoring merges with what is there; the newer version of each record is kept.

Where is my data?

Only on this iPhone. We have no server and cannot see anything you add. The app goes online only when you search for a city in Footprints (Apple Maps) and when you buy or restore All Templates (Apple's App Store). See the Privacy Policy.

How do I delete everything?

Settings → Delete all data (two confirmations). Export a backup first if you want to keep a copy.

宝宝月龄照 · 帮助

宝宝月龄照：看着天数一天天长，记下每个“第一次”，把照片做成能分享的月龄卡片。不用注册，没有广告，照片只留在手机上。

常见问题

天数和月龄怎么算？

出生当天是第 1 天。月龄按公历算：比如 1 月 10 日出生，2 月 10 日满 1 个月。31 号出生的宝宝，在没有 31 号的月份按当月最后一天算；2 月 29 日出生的宝宝，平年按 2 月 28 日算。

有哪些纪念日？

第 7 天、满月（第 30 天）、每个月龄日、百天、半岁、周岁和之后每年的生日、第 500 天、第 1000 天。打开“设置 → 纪念日提醒我”后，会在前一天和当天上午 9 点各提醒一次。

怎么做月龄照卡片？

在“今天”点“做月龄照”，选一张照片。标题会按照片的拍摄日期自动填好（拿不到拍摄日期时用今天），可以换模板和尺寸（3:4、1:1、9:16），拖动和双指缩放照片，改标题、名字、日期和一句话，然后分享或存到相册。

卡片角落的小字可以去掉吗？

可以。设置 → 关闭“卡片上显示‘用…制作’”。

哪些模板免费？“模板全集”是什么？

4 套模板免费（晨白、胶带手账、朱红印章、月龄表盘），天数、小组件、“第一次”、成长记录、足迹、备份等其他功能也全部免费。“模板全集”是一个一次性内购，不是订阅，买一次就能用其余模板和以后新增的模板。带小锁的模板可以先套上自己的照片预览，分享或存到相册时才会请你购买。付款由苹果的 App Store 处理，我们收不到也看不到任何付款信息。

换了手机或重装后，怎么恢复“模板全集”？

用同一个 Apple 账户登录后，设置 → 模板全集 → 恢复购买；购买页上也有“恢复购买”。

身高体重的折线是什么意思？

只是把你记下的数字连成线，方便回看。这里只做记录，不提供医学建议；有问题请咨询医生。

有两个宝宝怎么办？

设置 → 添加宝宝。首页点名字可以切换；每个小组件可以在编辑小组件时选显示哪个宝宝。

怎么换手机或给家人一份？

设置 → 导出备份，得到一个 ZIP 文件；在另一台手机上安装本 App 后，设置 → 从备份恢复。恢复时会和已有数据合并，同一条记录保留较新的版本。

数据存在哪里？

只存在这台手机上。我们没有服务器，看不到你的任何内容。联网只有两处：在“足迹”里用苹果地图搜索城市；购买或恢复“模板全集”时通过苹果的 App Store。详见隐私政策。

怎么删除全部数据？

设置 → 删除全部数据（需要确认两次）。删除前可以先导出备份。
