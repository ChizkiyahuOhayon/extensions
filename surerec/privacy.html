SureRec Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

SureRec does not collect, store, transmit or sell any personal data. Recordings and settings are stored only on your computer, inside Chrome's storage for the extension. Nothing is uploaded to any server. SureRec contains no analytics or tracking.

Uninstalling SureRec deletes its stored recordings; videos you downloaded stay in your Downloads folder.

Please get consent before recording other people (for example in online meetings) and follow local law.

SureRec 隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 10 月 9 日

SureRec 不收集、不保存、不传输、不出售任何个人数据。录像和设置只保存在你的电脑上（Chrome 为插件分配的存储空间中），不会上传到任何服务器。SureRec 没有任何统计或跟踪。

卸载 SureRec 会删除插件内保存的录像；已下载到“下载”文件夹的视频不受影响。

录制他人（例如线上会议）前，请先取得对方同意，并遵守当地法律。
