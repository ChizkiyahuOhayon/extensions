Face Compass · Help

Face Compass measures the 3D shape of your face with the front TrueDepth camera, shows your face shape and proportions such as three sections and five eyes, and suggests haircuts, glasses, hats and necklines. Then it uses your own clothes to suggest today's outfits for the weather, your mood and the occasion. Face measurement happens only on your iPhone and no face image is ever saved; clothes photos are kept only on your iPhone. While you scan, the screen shows only mesh lines.

Which devices work?

An iPhone with Face ID, on iOS 17 or later. iPhones without the TrueDepth camera (for example Touch ID models) can't scan; the colour quiz and the wardrobe still work.

How do I get a good scan?

  • Hold the phone at eye level, facing the screen, about 40 cm away.
  • Relax your face: no smile, mouth gently closed.
  • Even light on your face; avoid harsh direct light and very dark rooms.
  • The first time, scan three times in a row. After each one, lower the phone and raise it again, or just tap "Start scan N".

Why three scans?

Every scan has a little error. Results use the middle value of several scans, which is steadier than a single scan, and the face shape only changes when several scans clearly point elsewhere. With a different expression, light or distance, the total usually still moves by a few points between sessions; the more scans, the steadier it gets. Each later "Scan again" joins your profile.

What does "between X and Y" mean?

Your measurements sit near the line between two shapes, closer than the scan error, so the app doesn't force one. Suggestions start with what suits both.

What does the total mean?

It measures how close your facial proportions are to common reference proportions. It covers only one geometric side of appearance, is for reference only, says nothing about a person's worth, and is not a medical assessment.

Why was one scan "not merged"?

One of its ratios differed from your profile by more than about 10% (perhaps someone else, or an unusual pose), so it was left out to protect your profile. Me → Scan profile shows whether each scan was merged.

How is the undertone decided?

From your answers to five questions; the camera is not used. It is a self-assessment guide and you can retake it any time.

How do I add clothes?

In Wardrobe, tap Add, then take a photo, choose one from Photos or paste a picture. The app removes the background and finds the main colours on your iPhone. Pick the category (when the app is confident it preselects one for you to check), then tap Save. A flat or hanging piece against a plain background cuts out best. Category, thickness, occasions and seasons have defaults you can change any time.

How are today's outfits put together?

Layers and thickness follow the temperature, with changes for rain and wind; colours use a neutral base with at most two accents; pieces near the face favour colours that suit your undertone; necklines and hats follow your face shape; your mood sets how bold the colours are; and nothing you marked as worn in the last two days is repeated. Every outfit comes with its reasons. If an outfit can't be made, the app says what is missing.

Where does the weather come from?

If you allow location, your approximate location is used to get the local weather from Apple Weather. If not, or if it isn't available, pick the temperature, rain and wind yourself; the app remembers your last choice.

How does "To buy" work?

Add two to five pieces you're deciding between (shop pictures or screenshots are fine; price, note and link text are optional). For each one the app counts the new outfits it makes with what you own, checks its colour against your undertone, looks for something similar you already have and notes any gap it fills, then gives a suggestion. If you buy it, move it to your wardrobe in one tap.

What is the body-proportion quiz for?

Four optional questions that only adjust cut and colour-placement hints in outfits. It talks about visual proportions, never better or worse. You can skip it.

Where is my data, and how do I delete it?

Only on your iPhone, excluded from backups. Delete any piece from its page; Me → Settings → Delete all data removes scans, quizzes, clothes photos, wardrobe, candidates, outfit history and body answers; Settings → Withdraw face-information consent deletes data and turns scanning off. Deleting the app also deletes all data. The only network use is the weather, which sends only an approximate location. See the privacy policy.

个人造型师 · 帮助

个人造型师用 iPhone 的前置原深感摄像头测量脸部的三维形状，告诉你脸型、三庭五眼等比例，给出发型、眼镜、帽子和领口的建议；再用你自己的衣服，按天气、心情和场合推荐今日穿搭。人脸测量只在本机计算，不保存任何人脸影像；衣服照片只保存在本机。扫描时屏幕上只显示网格线。

需要什么设备？

带 Face ID 的 iPhone，iOS 17 或更新。没有原深感摄像头的 iPhone（例如用 Touch ID 的机型）不能扫描，冷暖问答和衣橱仍可用。

怎样扫得准？

  • 手机和眼睛同高，正对屏幕，距离约 40 厘米。
  • 表情放松，不要笑，嘴自然合上。
  • 脸上光线均匀，避开强烈直射光和很暗的环境。
  • 第一次连续扫 3 次。每次完成后，把手机放下再举起来，或直接点“开始第 N 次”。

为什么要扫 3 次？

一次扫描会有一点误差。结果取多次扫描的中间值，比单次更稳；脸型只有在多次都明确指向别的类型时才会改。不过表情、光线和距离不同，总分在不同时段通常还会上下浮动几分，扫的次数越多越稳。之后每次“再扫一次”都会并入档案。

“介于 X 和 Y 之间”是什么意思？

你的测量值在两种脸型的分界附近，差距小于扫描误差，App 不硬分成其中一种。建议会先给两者都适合的。

总分代表什么？

总分衡量的是你的面部比例和常见参考比例的接近程度。它只是外貌的一个几何侧面，仅供参考，不代表一个人的价值，也不是医学判断。

为什么有一次扫描“未并入”？

那次的某个比例和你的档案相差超过约 10%（可能换了人，或者那次姿势不对），为了不影响档案，没有并入。在“我的 → 扫描档案”能看到每次扫描是否并入。

冷暖是怎么判断的？

根据你回答的五个问题，不用摄像头。这是自我判断的参考，可以随时重答。

怎样把衣服放进衣橱？

在“衣橱”点“添加”，拍照、从相册选或粘贴图片。App 在本机去背景、找出主色。类别由你选一个（有把握时 App 会先帮你选上，你确认即可），然后点“保存”。平铺或挂在素色背景前拍，去背景效果最好。厚薄、场合、季节都有默认值，可以随时改。

今日穿搭是怎么配的？

按气温决定层数和厚薄，下雨大风会调整；颜色以中性色打底、最多两种强调色；靠近脸的单品优先用适合你冷暖的颜色；领口和帽子参考你的脸型；心情决定颜色鲜艳程度；不重复最近两天“穿了这套”的组合。每套都写了理由。配不出时会告诉你还缺哪一类。

天气从哪里来？

允许定位的话，用大致位置向苹果天气取当地天气；不允许或取不到时，自己选气温档、是否下雨和大风，App 会记住上次的选择。

“想买的”怎么用？

放进 2 到 5 件正在犹豫的单品（商品图、截图都行，可以填价格、备注、链接文字），App 会算每件能和已有的衣服组成几套、颜色是否适合你、是否和已有的重复、填补了什么空缺，然后给出建议。买了可以直接放进衣橱。

体型问答有什么用？

四个可选的问题，只用来调整穿搭里的版型和配色建议，只讲视觉比例，不讲好坏。可以跳过。

数据在哪里？怎么删除？

只在你的 iPhone 上，不进入备份。衣橱里每一件都可以单独删除；“我的 → 设置 → 删除全部数据”会删除扫描、问答、衣服照片、衣橱、候选、穿搭记录和体型答案；“设置 → 撤回人脸信息同意”会删除数据并停止扫描。删除 App 也会删除全部数据。唯一的联网是取天气，发出去的只有大致位置。详见隐私政策。
