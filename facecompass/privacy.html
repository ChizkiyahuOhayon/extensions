Face Compass Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 15, 2026

Face Compass (the "app") is provided by Arete Robotics. The app uses the front TrueDepth camera of your iPhone to measure the 3D shape of your face and shows your face shape, proportions and styling suggestions. You can also add photos of your own clothes to a wardrobe on your iPhone and get outfit suggestions for the weather, your mood and the occasion, or compare clothes you are thinking of buying. This policy explains what the app processes, how, and how you delete it.

1. What the app processes

Face information. During a scan, the app reads the front TrueDepth camera image and the 3D face mesh in real time through Apple's ARKit.

  • Everything is calculated on your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded or sent to us or to anyone else.
  • The camera image is used only to check whether the light is too dark or too bright, and is discarded immediately. It is never shown on screen and no photos or video are saved. While you scan, the screen shows only mesh lines.
  • The 3D mesh is used only to measure distances (for example face width and the gap between the eyes). The mesh is not saved.
  • What is saved on your iPhone: the measurement numbers (lengths and ratios), the middle value of a few mouth and brow expression coefficients for each scan (numbers from 0 to 1, such as how far the mouth corners are stretched, the lips pressed or the jaw open, used to check how expression affects the measurements), the date of each scan, and the face shape and scores calculated from them. No image of your face is ever saved.

Before the camera is used, the app asks for your separate consent to process face information. If you don't agree, scanning stays off; the rest of the app remains available.

Face data is never used for advertising, marketing or data mining.

Photos of your clothes. Pictures you take, choose from Photos or paste in the Wardrobe or To buy tabs:

  • The background is removed, main colours are found and a category is suggested on your iPhone; the processed picture is saved on your iPhone to show your wardrobe and build outfit pictures.
  • They are stored only on your iPhone and never uploaded.
  • Choosing from Photos uses the system photo picker: the app receives only the picture you pick and does not ask for access to your library.
  • Pasting reads only the picture you paste.
  • You can delete any piece with its photo, or everything at once.

Wardrobe and outfit information. Each piece's category, thickness, occasions, seasons and colours; your "wear this today" history (kept for up to a year); wish-list candidates with the price, note and link text you type (the link is stored as text only; the app never opens or reads it); and your body-proportion quiz answers. All of it stays on your iPhone.

Approximate location (only for the weather). If you allow location while using the app, the app asks only for an approximate location (reduced accuracy) and uses it to get today's local weather from Apple's weather service (WeatherKit). Only the approximate location is sent, and it is handled by Apple's weather service; the app does not store it or send it to us or anywhere else. Without location the app works normally and you pick the temperature yourself. This is the app's only network use.

Quiz answers and settings. Your colour-quiz answers, whether you have seen the welcome screen and agreed to the face-information notice, your hair-length choice and your last manual temperature pick are saved on your iPhone.

The app does not collect your name, phone number, email, account, contacts, device identifiers, usage analytics, crash logs or advertising identifiers. There is no account, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDK.

2. Where and how long

All data stays in the app's storage on your iPhone and is excluded from iCloud and computer backups. It is kept until you delete it.

3. How to delete

  • Open any piece in Wardrobe or To buy to delete it and its photo.
  • Me → Settings → Delete all data (two confirmations) removes all scans, your profile and quiz answers, and all clothes photos, wardrobe, wish list, outfit history, body answers and the remembered temperature.
  • Settings → Withdraw face-information consent deletes all data and turns scanning off until you agree again.
  • Deleting the app deletes all its data.

Because we never receive your data, we cannot look it up, export it or delete it for you; all of the above happens on your device.

4. Sharing

The face card contains your face shape, scores and the app name, with no photo and no face mesh. The outfit picture contains the photos of that outfit's clothes and the app name. You choose where they go in the system share sheet; the app never sends anything by itself.

5. Permissions

  • Camera: live measurement during a scan, and the system camera when you photograph clothes.
  • Location (while using, approximate): only for the local weather.
  • You can turn these off at any time in the iPhone's Settings.

6. Age

The app is for people aged 16 and over. We do not knowingly process information about anyone under 16.

7. What the results are

Face shape, the total and outfit suggestions are geometric measurements and styling references. The total measures how close your facial proportions are to common reference proportions. It covers only one geometric side of appearance, is for reference only, says nothing about a person's worth, and is not a medical assessment. The body-proportion quiz describes visual proportions only, never better or worse.

8. Changes

If the way the app handles data changes, we will update this policy and tell you in the app first, and ask for your consent again for any change involving face information.

个人造型师隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 15 日

个人造型师（以下称“本 App”）由 Arete Robotics 提供。本 App 用 iPhone 的前置原深感摄像头测量脸部的三维形状，给出脸型、比例和搭配建议；你也可以把自己的衣服拍照放进本机衣橱，让本 App 按天气、心情和场合推荐今日穿搭，或比较想买的衣服。本政策说明本 App 会处理哪些信息、怎么处理，以及你怎样删除。

一、我们处理哪些信息

1. 人脸信息（敏感个人信息）

扫描时，本 App 通过苹果 ARKit 实时读取前置原深感摄像头的画面和人脸三维网格。处理方式：

  • 只在你的 iPhone 上实时计算，不上传，不发送给我们或任何第三方。
  • 画面只用来判断光线是否太暗或太亮，算完立即丢弃，不显示在屏幕上，也不保存照片或视频。扫描时屏幕上只显示网格线。
  • 三维网格只用来量距离（例如脸宽、两眼间距），不保存网格。
  • 保存在本机的只有测量结果的数字（长度和比例）、每次扫描时几项嘴部和眉部表情系数的中间值（0 到 1 之间的数字，例如嘴角拉伸、抿嘴、下颌张开的程度，用来检查表情对测量的影响）、扫描日期，以及由这些数字得出的脸型和分数。不保存任何人脸影像。

在打开摄像头之前，本 App 会单独请你同意人脸信息的处理；你不同意，就不能扫描，其他页面仍可查看。

2. 你添加的衣服照片

你在“衣橱”或“想买的”里拍照、从相册选择或粘贴的图片：

  • 在本机去掉背景、提取主色、猜测类别，然后把处理后的图片保存在本机，用来显示衣橱和拼出穿搭图。
  • 只保存在你的 iPhone 上，不上传。
  • 从相册选择时使用系统的照片选择器，本 App 只拿到你选中的那一张，不申请访问整个相册。
  • 粘贴时只读取你主动粘贴的那一张图片。
  • 每一件都可以单独删除（连同照片），也可以一键全部删除。

3. 衣橱和穿搭信息

单品的类别、厚薄、场合、季节、颜色等属性；“今天穿这套”的记录（最多保留一年）；想买的候选，以及你填的价格、备注和商品链接文字（本 App 只把链接当文字保存，不打开、不读取）；体型问答的答案。都只保存在本机。

4. 大致位置（只在取天气时）

如果你允许本 App 在使用期间获取位置，本 App 只申请大致位置（降低精度），用来向苹果的天气服务（WeatherKit）获取你当地今天的天气。发出去的只有大致位置，由苹果的天气服务处理；本 App 不保存位置，也不把位置发给我们或任何其他地方。不允许定位也可以正常使用，自己选气温即可。这是本 App 唯一的联网。

5. 冷暖问答的答案、设置

冷暖问答的选择、是否看过欢迎页、是否同意人脸信息说明、发型长度选项、上次手动选择的气温等，保存在本机。

本 App 不收集：姓名、电话、邮箱、账号、通讯录、设备标识符、使用统计、崩溃日志、广告标识符。本 App 没有账号，没有广告，不做跟踪，不使用任何第三方 SDK。

二、存储位置和期限

  • 所有数据只存在你的 iPhone 上本 App 的沙盒里，并设为不进入 iCloud 或电脑备份。
  • 数据会一直保存，直到你删除。

三、怎样删除

  • 衣橱或想买的里，点进某一件可以删除这一件和它的照片。
  • 在本 App“我的 → 设置 → 删除全部数据”，两次确认后删除所有扫描、档案、问答结果，以及衣服照片、衣橱、想买的候选、穿搭记录、体型答案和记住的气温。
  • 在“设置 → 撤回人脸信息同意”，删除全部数据并停止扫描，直到你再次同意。
  • 删除本 App，所有数据随之删除。

因为我们从未收到你的数据，我们无法替你查询、导出或删除；以上操作都在你的设备上完成。

四、分享

“分享”生成的脸型卡片只包含脸型名称、分数和 App 名字，不含照片和人脸网格；穿搭分享图只包含你那套衣服的照片和 App 名字。图片由你自己在系统分享面板里选择去处，本 App 不会自动发送。

五、权限

  • 相机：扫描时的实时测量；给衣服拍照时使用系统相机。
  • 位置（使用期间，大致位置）：只用于取当地天气。
  • 你可以在 iPhone“设置”里随时关闭这些权限。

六、未成年人

本 App 面向 16 岁及以上的用户。我们不会有意处理 16 岁以下用户的信息。

七、结果的性质

脸型、总分和穿搭建议都是几何测量和搭配参考。总分衡量的是你的面部比例和常见参考比例的接近程度。它只是外貌的一个几何侧面，仅供参考，不代表一个人的价值，也不是医学判断。体型问答只描述视觉比例，不评价好坏。

八、政策变更

如果本 App 的数据处理方式发生变化，我们会先更新本政策并在 App 内提示，涉及人脸信息的变化会再次征求你的同意。
