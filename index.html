Company

Excellence, practised.

Aretē (ἀρετή) is the Greek word for excellence that comes from doing a thing well, again and again. Arete Robotics was founded in Dublin in 2026 to build the data and tools that let robots learn the way people do: by watching skilled work.

We label first-person video for robot learning, from public datasets or your own footage. Send us a few minutes and we will label it for free so you can judge the quality.
