Bring your own video

Send us a few minutes of video. We label them for free.

If your team films hands-on work, we turn that footage into robot training data. A hotel or facilities operator, for example, could send a recording of housekeeping or maintenance work. You judge the quality before you decide on more.

Freefor the first few minutes
Within a weekturnaround
Privateyour footage stays private

What we accept

  • Head-mounted or chest-mounted cameras.
  • Action cameras and phones.
  • A single camera or a stereo pair. With a single camera, depth is estimated from hand size. When you send the stereo calibration, we measure wrist depth by triangulation.
  • A few minutes of footage is enough to start.

What comes back

  • Hand keypoints in 2D and 3D, 21 per hand.
  • Wrist pose and grasp.
  • Sub-steps, each with a language instruction.
  • Files in LeRobot v3 format.
  • An overlay preview video.
  • A one-page quality report.

How to start

Email hello@arete-robotics.com with a download link, the camera model and where the camera was worn. We use your footage only to label it for you.

Company

Excellence, practised.

Aretē (ἀρετή) is the Greek word for excellence that comes from doing a thing well, again and again. Arete Robotics builds the data and tools that let robots learn the way people do: by watching skilled work.

We label first-person video for robot learning, from public datasets or your own footage. Send us a few minutes and we will label it for free so you can judge the quality.
