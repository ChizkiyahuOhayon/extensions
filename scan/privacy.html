Quietbit Scan Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Quietbit Scan is an iPhone and iPad app that makes 3D models of rooms and objects with the LiDAR scanner. It has no account and no server, makes no network connections, and does not collect, sell or share personal data.

What stays on your device

The camera and LiDAR scanner capture the shape and colour of what you scan. Each scan (the 3D model, its photos and a thumbnail) is saved only on your device, in the app's folder, which you can see in the Files app. Deleting a scan, or deleting the app, removes it.

When data leaves your device

Only when you choose to export or share a model. The file then goes wherever you send it (for example AirDrop, Files, Mail or another app), under that service's own terms. Arete Robotics never receives a copy.

What Quietbit Scan does not do

  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or telemetry.
  • No access to your location, contacts or photo library.
  • It does not sell or transfer data to anyone.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Quietbit Scan 隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 10 月 15 日

Quietbit Scan 是一款 iPhone 和 iPad 应用，用激光雷达（LiDAR）扫描仪给房间和物体做 3D 模型。它没有账号，没有服务器，不联网，不收集、不出售、不分享任何个人数据。

只保存在你设备上的内容

相机和激光雷达扫描仪会记录被扫描物体的形状和颜色。每次扫描（3D 模型、照片和缩略图）只保存在你的设备上，位于应用自己的文件夹，可以在"文件"App 里看到。删除扫描或删除应用，数据会一并删除。

数据什么时候会离开你的设备

只有当你主动导出或分享模型时。文件会发送到你选择的地方（例如隔空投送、"文件"、邮件或其他应用），适用对方服务的条款。Arete Robotics 不会收到任何副本。

Quietbit Scan 不做的事

  • 没有统计、跟踪、广告或遥测。
  • 不访问你的位置、通讯录或照片图库。
  • 不向任何人出售或转让数据。

儿童

本应用不收集任何人的数据，包括儿童。
