Quietbit Scan

Scan rooms and objects in 3D with the LiDAR scanner on iPhone Pro and iPad Pro. Everything is processed and stored on your device.

Export formats

  • USDZ for AR Quick Look, Reality Composer and Keynote
  • GLB for the web, Blender and most 3D apps
  • OBJ with textures, PLY and STL for 3D printing (choose the unit)
  • Robot simulation: a MuJoCo scene and an Isaac Sim USD scene with collision shapes, in one zip

Tips for a good scan

  • Move slowly and keep the scanned surface 0.5–3 m away.
  • Use even lighting; avoid mirrors, glass and very dark surfaces.
  • Walk around an object rather than turning it.

Questions and problems

Quietbit Scan

用 iPhone Pro 和 iPad Pro 上的激光雷达扫描仪，给房间和物体做 3D 扫描。所有处理和存储都在你的设备上完成。

导出格式

  • USDZ：用于 AR 快速查看、Reality Composer 和 Keynote
  • GLB：用于网页、Blender 和大多数 3D 软件
  • 带贴图的 OBJ，以及 PLY、STL（3D 打印，可选单位）
  • 机器人仿真：MuJoCo 场景和 Isaac Sim USD 场景，含碰撞体，打包成一个 zip

扫描技巧

  • 慢慢移动，扫描面保持在 0.5–3 米之内。
  • 光线均匀；避开镜子、玻璃和很暗的表面。
  • 扫物体时绕着它走，不要转动物体。
