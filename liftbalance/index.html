Lift Balance Support

Lift Balance is a workout log that compares your push, pull and legs on one scale and suggests extra work for the area that lags. It is for iPhone with iOS 17 or later. Your records stay on your device, and there is no account.

Getting started

Tap Get started and answer a few questions about your goal, training days and equipment. Entering recent best sets is optional. The app builds a plan and shows a first balance reading when it has enough data.

Logging a workout

On Today, tap Start workout. Tap the check button to complete a set; the rest timer starts on its own. Tap the “…” button on an exercise for the plate calculator and other options. Tap Finish when you are done. If you leave the app, you can resume the workout from Today.

How the balance reading works

For each exercise, the best set in the chosen window gives an estimated one-rep max (Epley formula, sets of 12 reps or fewer). Each exercise is converted to a bench-press equivalent with typical strength ratios. A body area’s number is the average of its exercises, and an area more than 10% below the strongest is marked weak. Log lifts for at least two of push, pull and legs to see a comparison. The ratios and strength levels are approximate references for training, not medical advice.

Next week’s plan

When an area is weak, Next week’s plan lists suggested changes. Nothing changes until you tap Apply to next week, and you can undo the last adjustment. You can also edit each day yourself.

Rest notifications and widgets

Allow notifications to get an alert when rest ends. Add Lift Balance from your iPhone’s widget gallery for the Home Screen or Lock Screen. If a widget looks out of date, open the app; iOS controls when widgets refresh.

Importing, exporting and deleting

Settings can import a CSV exported by other workout apps. Exercises are matched to the library by name, and unknown ones are kept as custom exercises. Settings can also export your data as CSV or as a full JSON backup. Save an export somewhere you control before you delete data or change phones.

Settings → Delete all data removes workouts, plan, bodyweight and settings from this iPhone. It cannot be undone, and there is no server copy for recovery.

Questions and problems

Write to hello@arete-robotics.com with your iPhone model, iOS version, app version and the steps to reproduce the issue.

力衡训练支持

力衡训练是一款训练记录 App，把推、拉、腿放在同一把尺子上比较，并给落后的部位提出加练建议。适用于 iOS 17 及以上版本的 iPhone。记录保存在本机，没有账号。

开始使用

点“开始”，回答几个关于目标、训练天数和器械的问题。最近的最好成绩可以不填。App 会生成计划，数据够的时候显示第一次力量画像。

记录一次训练

在“今天”页点“开始训练”。点对勾完成一组，休息倒计时会自动开始。点动作右侧的“…”可以打开杠铃片计算器和其他选项。练完点“结束”。中途离开 App 后，可以在“今天”页继续这次训练。

力量画像怎么算

时间窗内每个动作最好的一组，用 Epley 公式估算 1RM（只算 12 次以内的组），再按常见力量比例折算成卧推当量。每个部位取它各动作的平均值，比最强部位低 10% 以上的部位记为偏弱。至少记录推、拉、腿中两个部位的动作才能比较。比例和力量等级是训练用的近似参考，不是医疗建议。

下周计划

有部位偏弱时，“下周计划”会列出建议调整。点“应用到下周”后才会生效，也可以撤销上次调整。每一天的内容都可以自己改。

休息通知和小组件

允许通知后，休息结束时会收到提醒。在 iPhone 的小组件图库里添加力衡训练，可以放在桌面或锁屏。小组件看起来没更新时，打开一次 App；实际刷新时间由 iOS 控制。

导入、导出和删除

在设置里可以导入其他训练 App 导出的 CSV，按名称匹配动作库，匹配不上的作为自定义动作保留。也可以导出 CSV 或完整的 JSON 备份。删除数据或更换手机前，请先把导出文件保存到自己控制的位置。

设置中的“删除全部数据”会从这台 iPhone 上删除训练、计划、体重和设置，删除后无法恢复，也没有服务器副本。

问题反馈

请发邮件至 hello@arete-robotics.com，注明 iPhone 型号、iOS 版本、App 版本及复现步骤。
