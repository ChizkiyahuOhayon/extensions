Lift Balance Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Lift Balance keeps your training records on your iPhone. It has no account, no developer-operated server, no analytics, no tracking and no advertising, and the app makes no network connections. Arete Robotics does not receive your data.

What stays on your device

Your workouts, training plan, bodyweight entries and settings, including the optional sex setting used for strength levels, are saved in the app’s local storage.

A small summary is shared locally with the app’s widget through an App Group. It contains your sessions this week, weekly streak, next workout, push, pull and legs figures, and which area is weak. The widget does not open your workout database.

Rest alerts are local notifications scheduled on your device, and the rest timer can appear as a Live Activity. Your device’s backup settings may include app data in backups managed by Apple.

Importing, exporting and sharing

Files you import are read on your device. CSV and JSON exports and share cards leave the app only when you choose to save or share them through system controls. The destination you choose handles that data under its own terms, and we receive no automatic copy. Share cards can show your bodyweight only if you turn that on in Settings. If you email support, we receive the message and attachments you choose to send.

Deleting your data

Delete an individual workout, or use Settings → Delete all data to remove workouts, plan, bodyweight and settings from this iPhone. Deleting the app also removes its data. Deletion does not affect files you previously exported. We do not keep a server copy.

Children

Lift Balance is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

力衡训练隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 11 月 8 日

力衡训练将训练记录保存在你的 iPhone 上。没有账号、开发者运营的服务器、统计、跟踪或广告，App 也不联网。Arete Robotics 不会收到你的数据。

保存在本机的内容

训练记录、训练计划、体重记录和各项设置（包括用于计算力量等级的可选性别设置）都保存在 App 本地存储中。

一份小型汇总通过 App Group 在本机共享给小组件，内容包括本周训练次数、连续周数、下一次训练、推拉腿的数值和偏弱的部位。小组件不读取训练数据库。

休息提醒是在本机安排的本地通知，休息倒计时可以显示为实时活动。根据设备备份设置，App 数据可能包含在苹果管理的设备备份中。

导入、导出和分享

导入的文件只在本机读取。CSV、JSON 导出文件和分享卡片，只有你通过系统控件主动保存或分享时才会离开 App，适用所选接收服务的条款，我们不会自动收到副本。只有你在设置里打开后，分享卡片才会显示体重。如果你发送支持邮件，我们会收到你主动发送的内容和附件。

删除数据

可以删除单次训练，或在设置中“删除全部数据”，从这台 iPhone 上删除训练、计划、体重和设置。删除 App 也会删除其中的数据。删除不影响你之前已经导出的文件。我们没有服务器副本。

未成年人

力衡训练不面向儿童，也不收集任何人的数据。

政策更新

本政策如有变化，我们会更新此页面和上方日期。
